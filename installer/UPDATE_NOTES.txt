--------------------------------------------------
       RIFTCORE COMPANION - UPDATE v2.0.1
--------------------------------------------------

* Single Unified App in Task Manager:
  - Merged companion and in-game HUD under ONE clean
    process group ("RiftCore Companion").
  - Eliminated separate WebView2 Manager and Electron entries.

* Instant Alt-Tab Auto-Hide:
  - Zero-CPU event hook automatically hides the overlay
    when Alt-Tabbing out of League (to Discord/Browser/Desktop)
    and instantly restores it when returning to the game.

* Jungle Path Alignment:
  - Repositioned jungle clear route to float cleanly above
    champion ability icons and level-up buttons.

* Real-Time Ward & Trinket Synchronization:
  - Dynamically syncs with live item slot 6 trinket charges
    (showing exact remaining wards e.g. 1/2, 0/2, pink wards,
    and Oracle Lens sweeper status).

* High-Contrast White Timers:
  - Crisp, pure white timer digits (#ffffff) with dark drop
    shadows on all minimap objectives and spawn pit pins.

* Teammate Ults & Visual Fixes:
  - Reliable champion portraits with zero broken image icons.
