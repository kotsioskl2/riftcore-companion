--------------------------------------------------
       RIFTCORE COMPANION - UPDATE v2.1.1
--------------------------------------------------

* Champion Select Rune Editor:
  - Added separate Build and Runes tabs so the rune editor is not buried below the build guide.
  - Rebuilt rune editing as a compact two-column board with Riot's original rune and stat-shard art.
  - Kept META, BURST and SUSTAIN quick presets, plus clear secondary-row choices.
  - Replaced spell dropdowns with slot-based spell picks; selecting the other slot's spell swaps them.
  - Keeps rune choices valid when changing trees or rows and preserves each champion's setup.
  - APPLY RUNES + SPELLS sends the complete setup to the League Client.

--------------------------------------------------
       PREVIOUS UPDATE v2.1.0
--------------------------------------------------

* Champion Select:
  - Rebuilt champion selection as a searchable, role-filtered portrait roster with animated focus and selection states.
  - Follow the champion you hover or lock in through the League Client.
  - Keep custom runes, secondary rows, role and summoner spells per champion while switching between picks.
  - Restored META, BURST and SUSTAIN rune presets and the champion auto-sync control.

* Native in-game HUD:
  - Restored manual enemy summoner cooldowns and jungle camp respawn timers.
  - Added objective, spell and camp timer tabs plus a copy-to-chat call-out.
  - Moved minimap objective countdown tags off camp and route labels, and spaced ward suggestions to reduce collisions.
  - Added overlay settings for the new timer sections. Cooldown estimates use base spell values and do not infer summoner haste.
