--------------------------------------------------
       RIFTCORE COMPANION - UPDATE v1.1.1
--------------------------------------------------

Hey! Welcome to v1.1.1. Here is what you need to know:

* Full Screen In-Game HUD:
  Works natively over League of Legends in Full
  Screen mode with zero mouse lag and click-through.
  Hit Alt + G in-game anytime to show or hide it.

* Standby Overlay Guard:
  Automatically remains idle in Standby when you're
  not in a match, keeping your desktop clean.

* Tab Section Gold Difference:
  Shows real-time team gold lead/deficit and lane
  matchup difference right at the top scoreboard.

* Objective & Camp Timers:
  Clean floating dock above the minimap for Dragon,
  Baron, Voidgrubs, Blue Buff, and Red Buff.

* Small CS Score Tracker:
  Discreet CS score and CS/min pill beside your game
  clock with live matchup comparison.

* Skill Level-Up Indicator:
  Arrow directly above your ability bar showing your
  optimal skill upgrade for the next level.

Enjoy the climb! Simple, lightweight, and Vanguard-safe.
