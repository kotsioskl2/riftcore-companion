       RIFTCORE COMPANION - UPDATE v2.1.0

* Champion Select:
  - Rebuilt champion selection as a searchable, role-filtered portrait roster with animated focus and selection states.
  - Follow the champion you hover or lock in through the League Client.
  - Keep custom runes, secondary rows, role and summoner spells per champion while switching between picks.

* Native in-game HUD:
  - Restored manual enemy summoner cooldowns and jungle camp respawn timers.
  - Added objective, spell and camp timer tabs plus a copy-to-chat call-out.
  - Moved minimap objective countdown tags off camp and route labels, and spaced ward suggestions to reduce collisions.
  - Added overlay settings for the new timer sections. Cooldown estimates use base spell values and do not infer summoner haste.
