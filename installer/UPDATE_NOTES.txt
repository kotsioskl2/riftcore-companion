                  RIFTCORE COMPANION - UPDATE v1.1.0
======================================================================

Hey! Welcome to v1.1.0. Here is what you need to know:

- Full Screen In-Game HUD:
  Works natively over League of Legends in Full Screen mode!
  Zero mouse lag, clicks pass straight into the game.
  Hit Alt + G in-game anytime to show or hide it.

- Tab Section Gold Difference:
  Shows real-time team gold lead/deficit and lane matchup
  difference right at the top center scoreboard.

- Objective & Camp Timers:
  Separate floating timers on the map for Dragon, Baron,
  Voidgrubs, Blue Buff, and Red Buff.

- Small CS Score Tracker:
  Discreet CS score and CS/min pill beside your game clock
  with live matchup comparison.

- Skill Level-Up Indicator:
  Arrow directly above your ability bar showing your optimal
  skill upgrade for the next level.

Enjoy the climb! Simple, lightweight, and 100% Vanguard-safe.
