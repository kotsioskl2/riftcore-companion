--------------------------------------------------
       RIFTCORE COMPANION - UPDATE v1.2.2
--------------------------------------------------

Hey! Welcome to v1.2.2 Here is what is new:

* In-App Automatic Updates:
  Never miss a patch! RiftCore now checks GitHub
  Releases automatically and lets you download and
  upgrade with 1 click without ever opening a browser.

* Pure Native Rust Engine (90% Smaller):
  Completely removed legacy Electron bloat! The app
  now runs 100% natively on Rust and Windows WebView2,
  dropping the installer to just ~5MB with zero RAM waste.

* Vanguard Safe & Zero Delay:
  Compliant with Riot Games third-party policies.
  Reads official local client memory/LCU APIs only.

* Full Screen In-Game HUD:
  Works natively over League of Legends in Full
  Screen mode with zero mouse lag and click-through.
  Hit Alt + G in-game anytime to show or hide it.

* Live Matchup & Objective Timers:
  Shows team gold diff, lane matchup CS leads, and
  floating objective timers right above the minimap.

Enjoy the climb! Simple, lightweight, and Vanguard-safe.
