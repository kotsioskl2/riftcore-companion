Hey! Welcome to v1.1.0. Here is what you need to know:

• Overlay is Fixed: It now stays on your screen properly.
  Hit Alt + G in-game anytime to show or hide it.

• What to Level & Buy: Shows your next skill upgrade and exactly
  how much gold you need for your next item.

• Enemy Timers: Tap an enemy Flash or Ult to track their cooldown,
  or copy it straight to your team chat.

• Lane Tips: Quick reminders on how to beat your matchup.

Quick tip: Set League's video setting to 'Borderless' so the overlay
floats smoothly over your game.

That's it! Simple, fast, and 0 lag.
